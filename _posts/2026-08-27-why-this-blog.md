title: "Why this blog exists"
---

I keep a running log of the bugs I fix in open source. Mostly the deep kind: races, deadlocks, state machine edge cases — bugs that take days to reproduce and five lines to fix.

Two reasons for writing them up. First, writing forces me to actually understand the root cause instead of pattern-matching a patch. Second, the next person who touches that code path deserves the story while it's still fresh.

Each post links the merged PR it came from. No fluff, no listicles.
